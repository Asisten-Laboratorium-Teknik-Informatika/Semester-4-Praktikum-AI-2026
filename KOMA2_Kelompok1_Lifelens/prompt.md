# Panduan Prompt Ekspresi + Pose RINA (Image-to-Image)

Tinggal *copy-paste* teks di bawah ini ke AI Generator-mu. Prompt ini sudah mencakup **ekspresi wajah** sekaligus **pose & postur badan** agar RINA kelihatan hidup!

### 1. Idle (Diam / Menunggu)
> `neutral face, gentle smile, relaxed posture, arms resting down, sitting straight, looking at viewer, simple white background`

### 2. Happy (Senang / Menyapa)
> `happy face, big warm smile, closed eyes smile, bright expression, slightly tilting head, one hand waving slightly or touching chest, excited posture, simple white background`

### 3. Empathy (Empati / Ikut sedih)
> `empathetic expression, slightly sad smile, soft caring eyes, gentle gaze, leaning forward slightly, both hands clasped together near chest, comforting posture, simple white background`

### 4. Thinking (Berpikir / Loading)
> `thinking face, looking slightly up and to the side, finger on chin, slightly parted lips, leaning back slightly, pondering posture, simple white background`

### 5. Surprised (Kaget)
> `surprised expression, slightly widened eyes, slightly open mouth, pulling back slightly, hands raised slightly in surprise, concerned posture, simple white background`

### 6. Talking (Berbicara)
> `talking, open mouth, gentle smile, looking at viewer, one hand making a conversational gesture (like explaining something), active posture, simple white background`

### 7. Listening (Mendengarkan)
> `listening intently, gentle closed mouth smile, focused attentive eyes, leaning forward slightly, chin resting on hands or hands on lap, attentive posture, simple white background`

### 8. Concerned (Khawatir)
> `deeply concerned expression, worried eyes, slightly furrowed brows, leaning forward, hands reaching out slightly or clasped tightly, comforting posture, simple white background`

### 9. Encouraging (Menyemangati)
> `encouraging smile, confident look, determined soft expression, leaning forward, one hand clenched into a small fist cheering gesture, energetic posture, simple white background`

### 10. Neutral (Biasa)
> `calm expression, professional but friendly, gentle closed mouth smile, sitting straight, hands neatly on lap, relaxed formal posture, simple white background`
